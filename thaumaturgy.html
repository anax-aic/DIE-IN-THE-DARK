01

INTRODUCTION

Doing the Impossible

Every culture on Earth has its belief in 'magic': a force that exists outside the world, which can make amazing, dangerous or terrible things happen. Over countless years, numerous techniques have emerged as humanity explored different ways to harness the Unnatural - ranging from Sarkic carnomancy to psionics to demon-summoning. Collectively, the Coalition refers to these techniques as 'thaumaturgy', and their practice is considered taboo.

Like with most forms of the Unnatural, the usage of thaumaturgy is risky. Most thaumaturges have a short career and end up witless and divorced from reality, a 'John Doe' abandoned to any one of the many mental health facilities dotting the United States, mind (and sometimes body) torn at the seams by whatever lies beyond. Despite this, thaumaturgy has drawn great interest from the Coalition's members: from PISCES' attempts to harness psychic potential to the Program's use of Predator drones to trace ritual circles in the sky, it is both an open secret and a dark art kept off the books and suffocated behind endless layers of black marker.

A thaumaturgic 'spell', called a 'working', is primary carried out through rituals: chants, thoughts, motions, or preparations that connect the mind to powers from the outside. In this way, the ritual is just a means to an end for the thaumaturge - a single ritual can have countless variants in how its actually performed. Likewise, anything with intelligent thought can learn and utilize thaumaturgy.

What is a working?

A thaumaturgical spell, more formally called a working, is typically carried out through a ritual: a sequence of chants, thoughts, movements, preparations, or other actions that establishes a connection to forces beyond ordinary reality.

The ritual is a means to an end. A single working may have countless variations in how it is performed, provided its essential requirements are met. Any sufficiently intelligent being may be capable of learning and employing thaumaturgy.

CLASSIFICATION

Archetypes of Thaumaturgy

The Coalition currently recognizes the following broad archetypes. These are descriptive classifications, not mutually exclusive schools.

ARCHETYPE 01

Carnomancy

The manipulation of flesh and the body, a practice supposedly passed to humanity by the Grand Karcist Ion.

ARCHETYPE 02

Demonics

An offshoot of ancient theurgy concerned with summoning, binding, or channeling demons and other eldritch entities.

ARCHETYPE 03

Fae

Believed by some to represent the original practice of magic, typically associated with illusions, bargains, and hexes.

ARCHETYPE 04

Psionics

Phenomena involving the mind and psyche, theorized by some to be an innate human potential that remains repressed.

ARCHETYPE 05

Theurgy

The invocation of higher powers to produce miracles, commonly associated with Enochian language and related traditions.

04

PROCEDURE

Learning a Working

Thaumaturgy is accessed through a learned ritual or an object imbued with a ritual's effects. Workings are most often learned from books, recovered records, or other practitioners.

To a psychologically intact mind, ritual instructions may appear to be meaningless marks and incoherent fragments. Comprehension requires a profound reorientation of one's understanding of reality.

!
PLAYER MECHANICS

To learn a working, the Agent must devote the required study time to it and fail a SAN check. Study time is measured in out-of-character time. During the study period, the character must remain uninterrupted and focused on the material.

The character cannot be deployed on missions or undertake other activities that conflict with the study. Once the process is complete, the Agent learns the working and gains a general understanding of its effect.

05

EXECUTION

Activating a Working

To activate a thaumaturgical working, the operator must know the working and pay the costs required to produce its effect.

01

Identify

Confirm that the operator knows the working.

02

Prepare

Gather any required materials, instructions, and conditions.

03

Pay the cost

Determine the working's listed activation costs.

04

Roll

Roll 1d100 against the character's skill for that working.

05

Resolve

Apply the result and its associated costs or consequences.

!
PLAYER MECHANICS

Each character has a percentage skill rating for a particular working, starting at 33%. Roll 1d100. A result equal to or below the skill rating succeeds.

On success, the working takes effect and the operator pays its listed costs.

+
HANDLER GUIDANCE

A Handler may award a +20 percentage-point bonus to an Agent who has prepared with a copy of the instructions, detailed notes, or appropriate materials.

06

CONTINGENCIES

Failure & Cost

A failed activation does not necessarily leave the operator unharmed. The connection has been attempted; the operator must still contend with its consequences.

✓ SUCCESS

The working manifests

The effect occurs. The operator pays the working's listed costs.

× FAILURE

The connection falters

No effect occurs. The operator pays half the listed cost and loses a reduced amount of SAN, according to the game's rules.

! FORCED SUCCESS

Break the barrier

The operator may permanently sacrifice 1 POW to force the working to succeed.

!
PLAYER MECHANICS

When activation fails, the operator chooses between accepting the failed result and its reduced costs or permanently sacrificing 1 POW to force success. Apply the appropriate SAN loss and other costs for the chosen outcome.

Working through an object

Some objects carry the effects of a working. When activating such an object, the operator does not need to know the underlying ritual or make a Ritual Activation roll; they must instead pay the applicable cost and follow the object's activation requirements.

Thaumaturgical objects may have a finite number of uses. Once those uses are exhausted, the object loses its anomalous properties.

ILLUSTRATIVE PROCEDURE

Of the Shadowe

EXAMPLE ONLY

Lucas has studied Of the Shadowe, a working he deciphered in De Vermis Mysteriis. It allows him to reach out with a shadow limb to any target within sight and inflict 1d6 damage.

Once activated, the limb remains present until used. Afterward, it burns out after 1d4 turns. Activation costs 12 WP, 9 HP — caused by ritual cuts on his arms and wracking convulsions — and 1d10 SAN.

SKILL45%
WILLPOWER12 WP
PHYSICAL9 HP
SANITY1d10 SAN
Reveal activation resolution

Lucas rolls 31 on 1d100. Since 31 is below his 45% skill rating, activation succeeds. The shadow limb manifests, and Lucas pays the listed costs.

Had he failed, he would instead have paid 6 WP, 5 HP, and 1d4 SAN — or sacrificed 1 POW permanently to force the working to succeed.
